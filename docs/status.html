What the evidence says

NAMLNK23 executes 12,335 compiled audio callbacks across the offline USB/NAM, stock-chain and dual-pitch suites. Representative effects, 64 stock type switches, bypass skipping, both MIDI paths, seven-color cold/warm recall and global buffer persistence pass. All peripherals and timing are modeled; this is not a physical latency or recovery measurement.

Mac 0.5.8 and phone 0.8.7 recognize NAMLNK23. Mac Settings links to the official NUX downloads, builder and installation guide. Bundled starters are recognized by exact model identity without replacing existing Mac captures.

Compiled NAMLNK22 checks cover 1,652 USB/NAM callbacks and 2,501 combined-chain callbacks with two pitch blocks, NAM and Spring. The local package’s seven colors match the owner’s Mac presets. Native pitch comparisons match exactly; hardware deadlines remain unmeasured.

Earlier physical trials demonstrated NAM listening, selected delay/reverb combinations, seven-color power-off recall and phone live controls. The NAMLNK20 baseline exposed the USB audio problem. NAMLNK21/22 repair it offline; Logic input/output still needs a device test.

Public starter presets

Three RiffSmith models passed 4,800 compiled ARM callbacks, emulated cold boot and six physical-button-handler transitions. Each was also compared for 48,000 samples against the independent NAM reference; maximum error was below 0.00002. These are offline checks with modeled peripherals.

NAMLNK23 was installed on the development unit, where three private captures were transferred, saved and recalled, and the user confirmed guitar audio. The new public starter-bank variant has not been flashed or listened to. Physical timing, power-cycle persistence for this variant and Logic duplex operation remain unverified.

NAMLNK23 evidence →

Bluetooth playback

Local builder 0.3.1 includes NAMLNK23 r2, restoring the independent stereo phone-music path. The owner reports successful playback on the development unit. Stereo routing, guitar isolation and the three public starters pass offline checks; this is not broad device certification. Mac 0.5.8 and phone 0.8.7 need no update.

Looper development trial

NAMLNK25 recorded and played a loop through three preset colors on the development plug. The owner tested NAMLNK26 phone recording/playback but reported scratchy 30-second overdubs and missed physical PRESET finishes. NAMLNK27 addresses the codec block boundaries and redundant button debounce, and supplies live input level on the threshold slider in Mac 0.5.8 and phone 0.8.7. These fixes pass offline checks; their hardware/listening trial is pending. Optional 60-second storage has reduced bandwidth. The public builder continues to produce NAMLNK23 r2.

Known limits

A2 Lite at 48 kHz only; full A2 and arbitrary architectures are unsupported. Heavy effect combinations may exceed deadlines. No complete bootloader backup, guaranteed recovery, firmware-preserving update, stable release certification or looper is claimed.
