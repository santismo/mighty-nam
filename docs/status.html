02 / EVIDENCE

Results with their boundaries.

252preset storage cases
7slots recalled in ARM tests
1,557native wire checks

Offline work includes interrupted writes, corruption fallback, cold/warm startup, two saved A2 Lite model comparisons, stock button-branch emulation, and Dart/Swift/ARM request/reply checks. App and shared protocol tests passed.

These results do not prove physical flash writes, cache behavior, LED behavior, full-chain deadlines, or NAMLNK10 recovery. Real guitar listening has been observed on NAMLNK08. NAMLNK10 now passes seven-slot save, recall and power-off readback on the development unit, with button/LED cycling reported by the user. These physical results do not establish flash endurance or full-chain deadlines.

03 / NEXT GOALS

More from the same plug.

NEXT · STANDALONE

A looper under the PRESET button.

Hold to arm, start recording when the guitar crosses a threshold, then tap to close the loop and play over it. Include pre-roll and LED feedback. First, audit recording memory and processing headroom; loop duration is not yet established.

NEXT · IPHONE FIRST

Your controller, in your pocket.

Browse captures and edit the chain from your phone. Validate Bluetooth and wired control/model transfer on real hardware before choosing the delivery path. Shared code exists; a working iPhone release does not yet.

BEFORE A STABLE RELEASE

Extend the physical preset trial.

Seven-slot storage, power-off recall with the Mac closed, and button/color cycling now pass. Continue with NAMLNK10 listening, broader workloads, and return to updater mode.

04 / LIMITS

What we haven’t established.

  • General compatibility with every NAM architecture or sample rate.
  • Real-time safety for every heavy effect combination.
  • NAMLNK10 recovery re-entry and long-term storage endurance.
  • Preserving onboard captures through firmware reinstallation.
  • A complete bootloader backup or guaranteed USB rescue.
  • A fully independent, open replacement for all NUX firmware.
  • Looper duration, iPhone transport support, or an App Store release.

Updates to this page should follow evidence, not feature names. The current release is a development preview.
