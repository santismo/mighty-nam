A FAMILIAR WAY TO PLAY

Less between you
and your sound.

01

Find a capture. Make it yours.

Sign in to TONE3000, browse compatible captures, then load one into NAM. Keep several favorites ready in your Mac library.

02

Keep the effects you use.

Shape your chain with the stock gate, compressor, drive, cabinet, EQ, modulation, delay, and reverb. Select a block to edit it; click its symbol to bypass it.

03

Seven colors. Seven presets.

The new onboard bank pairs each color with a capture and its effects. Implementation is complete; standalone recall still needs the NAMLNK10 hardware trial.
